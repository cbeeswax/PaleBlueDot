# AI / Super Intelligence (SI) Use Disclosure

This project was built with the help of an AI coding assistant — **Claude Code**
(Anthropic's Claude, a large language model) — under the direction and review of
the team (Daibik, under the active supervision of Chandan). In line with the NASA
Space Apps Challenge rules on Super Intelligence (SI) tools, here is exactly where
and how SI was used.

## Where SI was used

- **Code** — The application's source code (React + TypeScript front-end, Express +
  TypeScript API, and the build/deploy configuration) was written with AI
  assistance, then directed, reviewed, and tested by the team. This is also
  recorded in version-control **metadata**: many git commits carry a
  `Co-Authored-By: Claude …` trailer.
- **Illustrations (inline SVG)** — The in-app vector artwork (the launch rocket,
  the Voyager spacecraft, the Deep Space Network scene, and the card icons) was
  authored as hand-written **SVG/CSS code** with AI assistance. These are drawn in
  code, not produced by an image generator.
- **Written content** — The README and the Space Apps submission write-ups were
  drafted with AI assistance and edited by the team.
- **Presentation** — The demo deck (`docs/PaleBlueDot-Demo.pptx`) was generated
  with AI assistance; its visuals are screenshots of the app and the app's own SVG
  art. The file's metadata notes the AI assistance.

## What SI was NOT used for

- **No AI image, photo, or video generation.** Every illustration is hand-coded SVG;
  every photograph is authentic, public-domain **NASA/JPL** imagery, shown with
  credit in the app.
- **No AI/SI audio generation.** All sound is produced live in the browser with the
  **Web Audio API** (simple tones), and narration uses the browser's built-in
  **SpeechSynthesis**.

## NASA branding

No NASA logos, flags, or mission identifiers appear in any AI-generated content.
The only NASA material used is authentic public-domain NASA/JPL photography,
displayed with its credit.
